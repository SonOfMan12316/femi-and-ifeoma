# UI/UX Standards

This project should maintain a premium, modern design language.

## Design Principles

- Apple, Linear, Stripe, Vercel quality.
- Minimal, elegant, functional.
- Prioritize usability over aesthetics.
- Consistent 8px spacing system.
- Strong typography hierarchy.
- Excellent whitespace.
- Accessible contrast (WCAG AA).
- Responsive across all devices.
- Smooth, subtle animations.
- Avoid generic AI-generated designs.

## Avoid

- Random floating cards
- Heavy glassmorphism
- Excessive gradients
- Huge empty hero sections
- Poor information hierarchy
- Overly rounded everything
- "Vibe-coded" layouts

## Components

Every component should:
- Have a clear purpose.
- Be reusable.
- Be accessible.
- Be visually balanced.
- Follow modern design patterns.

When modifying existing components, preserve business logic and functionality while improving layout, spacing, typography, responsiveness, and interaction quality.
