# Fémi & Ifeoma Cat Café — Brand Summary

## Who They Are

Fémi & Ifeoma Cat Café is Lagos's first cat café a lifestyle destination built around the calming presence of resident cats, good coffee, and community. In a city that rarely slows down, it exists as a deliberate pause: warm, intentional, and memorable.

**Website:** www.femiandifeomacatcafe.com
**Instagram:** @femiandifeoma
**Location:** Lagos, Nigeria
**Hours:** Monday – Saturday, 10AM – 5PM GMT+1
**CEO:** Daniel Omotayo

---

## Brand Positioning

Not just a café. Not just a cat shelter. A lifestyle destination built around moments, connection, and calm.

> "Because here, we don't just serve coffee — we create experiences that linger long after you've left."

## Brand Personality

| Trait | Expression |
|---|---|
| Warm | Inviting copy, cream backgrounds, soft imagery |
| Playful | Cat puns, expressive typography, whisker motifs |
| Community-driven | "Whether you're meeting friends, spending time with family…" |
| Calm | Intentional whitespace, unhurried tone |
| Boutique | Refined editorial touches alongside the playfulness |

---

## Brand Voice

**Do:**
- Friendly and welcoming
- Conversational ("you'll always find comfort")
- Playful cat puns (purrfect, purr, treat time)
- Bold, short statements for headlines

**Avoid:**
- Corporate or clinical language
- Luxury-for-luxury's-sake tone
- Passive or overly formal phrasing

**Copy examples:**
- "Lagos Moves Fast. We Don't."
- "Playtime, but make it PURRFECT."
- "Moments at the Café."
- "Treat Time!"
- "Relax, Purr and Community."

---

## Logo

Custom handcrafted lettering using **Let's Coogi** typeface. Feline details are embedded directly into the typography — paw prints replace letter dots, whisker-like strokes extend from the wordmark. A secondary mark exists: the logo inside an oval paw-print badge.

The logo is warm, fun, and unmistakably cat-centric.

## Target Audience

- Lagosians aged 18–40 seeking a social but calm escape
- Cat lovers, pet parents, and the cat-curious
- Friend groups and couples looking for memorable outings
- Remote workers wanting a relaxed third space
- Content creators (highly visual brand, strong social presence)

---

## Brand Challenge

Lagos is fast-moving and offers few true spaces to pause. The challenge: create a destination that feels less like a café and more like a peaceful retreat — through the calming presence of cats.

---

## Brand Solution

A visual identity rooted in calm, warmth, and connection — playful orange balanced by softer tones, expressive typography balanced by clean readability, hand-drawn cat illustrations balanced by editorial restraint.
